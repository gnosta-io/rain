How this is calculated
  • Gauges are CoCoRaHS volunteer stations inside the ZIP's Census boundary (tigerweb.geo.census.gov), most active first. A gauge's morning report covers the previous 24 hours.
  • Each gauge's MTD/YTD totals only the days it reported; its completeness is shown under its name.
  • Each gauge is compared with the model over the days it reported this year. A gauge more than 10% above or below the model is excluded from every average (it stays listed, struck through). The check is skipped until the model has at least 0.25" on those days, since tiny totals make percentages meaningless.
  • The averages are built day by day from whichever sources reported that day, so a missed day counts as no data rather than no rain. They run through yesterday, the last complete day.
  • The model is Open-Meteo's historical reanalysis at the ZIP's center point.
  • Normals are NOAA 1991–2020 averages (via data.rcc-acis.org) from the nearest station that has them, so treat them as a guide.
  • Forecast and alerts: National Weather Service (api.weather.gov). Radar: RainViewer. Map: Esri, HERE, Garmin, © OpenStreetMap contributors. ZIP lookup: Zippopotam.us.
